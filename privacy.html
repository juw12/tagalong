Tagalong privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. Applies to the web app, the Android and iPhone apps and the Windows app.

The short version

Tagalong has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. The people who make it never receive your trips, names, expenses or photos.

What is stored, and where

Your trips, the names you type, votes, places, expenses and cover photos are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Removing the app or clearing its site data deletes them. A backup file is only made when you ask for one, and it is saved where you choose.

When something leaves your device

  • Invite links and backup files. An invite link holds the whole trip inside the link itself. It goes wherever you send it, so treat the link like the trip.
  • Live sync, only if you turn it on. Changes are encrypted on your device (AES-GCM) with a key that only travels inside your invite links, then passed through free public message relays run by other organisations (broker.emqx.io, broker.hivemq.com, test.mosquitto.org). Those relays can see your internet address and encrypted data. They cannot read the trip. They keep the latest encrypted copy so that someone who was offline can catch up. Turning live sync off stops the sending.
  • Map links. Tapping MAP opens Google Maps with the place name you typed. Google's own policy applies from there.

Camera and photos

The receipt scanner reads the picture on your device. The picture is not uploaded. Camera and photo access is asked for only when you use the scanner or pick a cover photo.

Hosting

The web app is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your internet address when the app is downloaded. See GitHub's privacy statement for what it keeps. After the first visit the app works offline.

Children

Tagalong does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
