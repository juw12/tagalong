# Tagalong rubric v1 (council format; wording identical to the frozen rubric in refine_log.md)

Context: Tagalong is a free, installable, offline web app (PWA). It is meant to beat two products at once:
"Otw: Group Trip Planner" (App Store; clock or Morning/Afternoon/Evening day timelines, free gaps, drag and drop,
shortlist, invite by link with roles, expenses even or by item with tax + tip, receipt scan, currencies, balances,
settle up, saved-place collections, Google Maps links, offline viewing, sign-in required) and Traveler
(trytraveler.com: "countries, cities, return visits, flights, photos, friends, and road trips together in one
private travel history"). Judge only what the artifact text and screenshots show. Hard gates are code-checked
separately; do not re-score them.

- C1 Group planning beats Otw (w=0.30): 4 = itinerary and list only, 7 = parity on timelines, shortlist, moving events, sharing, 10 = parity plus things Otw does not list (voting, no account, any platform, undo)
- C2 Money (w=0.25): 4 = even split only, 7 = even/shares/itemised with tax and tip, currencies, balances, 10 = also fewest-transfers settle-up, exact rounding, receipt text paste, clear "who owes whom" at a glance
- C3 Travel history beats Traveler's pitch (w=0.15): 4 = a list of past trips, 7 = countries, cities, return visits, transport, companions, 10 = also a real world map, builds itself from finished trips, private by design
- C4 Craft and identity (w=0.20): 4 = generic template look, 7 = consistent scale, one accent, good empty states, light + dark, 10 = a distinct identity a designer would sign, flawless at every width
- C5 First-run clarity (w=0.10): 4 = blank screen, 7 = obvious first action, 10 = a newcomer plans, splits and shares within two minutes without reading help
