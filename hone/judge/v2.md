# Tagalong v2 (artifact for the judge)

Tagalong is a free, installable, offline web app (PWA). No account, zero runtime dependencies. New in this
version: optional live sync over free public relays, and free native builds (a signed Android APK and an
unsigned iPhone IPA).
The four attached images are real screenshots of this version: (1) phone 390 px: first run, plan tab, ideas
tab, money tab, passport in dark mode; (2) phone 390 px, the new screens: places tab, group tab, the
"Add expense" sheet scrolled to "Scan a receipt"; (3) desktop 1280 px first run; (4) desktop 1280 px passport.
The sample trip in the screenshots is demo data that ships with the app (`?demo=1`).

## What this version does

Planning
- Trips with one or more stops and a day-by-day itinerary. Each day is loose (Morning / Afternoon / Evening)
  or on the clock, chosen per day.
- Clock days show the free gaps between events; one tap fills a gap.
- Drag an event to another day or block, or move it from the edit sheet (keyboard and touch).
- Ideas shortlist with votes; the most wanted idea sorts first; one tap schedules it onto a day.
- New: Places tab. Save places as you find them and sort them into collections (food, sights, a friend's
  tips); each has a map link and one tap turns a place into an idea the group can vote on.
- Every delete has Undo.
- Invite link carries the whole trip (no sign-in). Opening someone else's link merges: newest edit of each
  item wins, votes add up. New: the Invite button sits in the trip header on every tab, not only on Group.
- New: live sync, off until someone turns it on. Every change then reaches the group within seconds while
  they are online, and the next time they open the app if not. It runs over three free public MQTT relays,
  encrypted end to end (AES-GCM) with a key that travels only inside the invite link. The Group tab shows
  how many relays answered ("Live - 2 of 3 relays").
- New: roles. Organizers edit the trip, people and roles, and can lock the plan. With the lock on, only
  organizers change days and times; everyone can still add ideas, vote and log expenses. Roles are kept by
  the app on every copy, not enforced by cryptography.
- Calendar export (.ics). Map link per event.
- Phone day strip: a fade and an arrow button appear while later days sit off-screen; the arrow scrolls on.
- First run shows a luggage-tag preview of the sample trip (one plan, one vote, one settle line). New: the
  tag is a button ("Open this trip") that opens the sample trip.

Money
- Even split, split by shares, or itemised with tax and tip spread in proportion.
- New: "Scan a receipt" takes a photo and reads it on the device (OCR, nothing uploaded) into line items.
  Pasting receipt text still works, and the read text can be checked and corrected before saving.
- Any currency per expense with the rate actually paid.
- Balances per person, and the fewest transfers that settle everyone, each with "Mark paid".
- New: "gets back" is printed in ink and only "owes" uses the accent, so the screen has one accent colour.
- Integer minor units throughout, so parts always add up to the bill.

Passport (travel history)
- Finished trips become a history by themselves: countries, cities, trips, days away, return visits, flights,
  road trips, companions, country stamps, a world map (been there / planned), a trip log.
- "Log a past trip" for trips taken before installing. One cover photo per trip.
- The map opens zoomed to the countries you have been to or planned on a phone, whole world on wide screens,
  with a link to switch. New: free pan and pinch zoom on the map, plus + and - buttons.
- Kept in the browser's storage only; backup and restore as one JSON file.

Getting it
- Web app at one URL, installable from the browser on any platform, works offline.
- New: a signed Android APK and an unsigned iPhone IPA, both built by free CI and attached to the release
  with checksums.

## Known weaknesses of this version (honest)

- Live sync depends on free public relays that promise no uptime. If all three are down, the app falls back
  to link merge. Behind a strict office proxy only one of the three relays is reachable.
- Roles and the plan lock are enforced by the app, not by cryptography: someone who edits their own copy by
  hand can ignore them.
- Receipt scan quality depends on the photo; a crumpled or dim receipt needs correcting by hand.
- No App Store or Google Play listing. The iPhone IPA has to be sideloaded and re-signed every 7 days with
  a free Apple ID; most iPhone users will use the web app instead.
- The native builds were checked on Android emulators and an iOS simulator, not on a physical phone.
- The Group tab is long on a phone: invite, live sync, people and roles stack in one column.

## Code-checked evidence (not for re-scoring)

- Unit tests 17/17: splits sum exactly to the bill, balances sum to zero, settle-up clears every balance,
  merge is commutative and keeps the newest edit, share link round-trips, sync messages encrypt and decrypt.
- Browser gates 9/9 on 8 viewports, light and dark: no console errors, no horizontal overflow 320 to 1440 px,
  WCAG AA contrast, tap targets >= 44 px, no text under 12 px, offline start, end-to-end flow incl. share link
  opened in a clean profile, money rows add up to the total.
- Feature checks 43/43 (roles, plan lock, places, receipt scan entry, map gestures, invite on every tab),
  locally and against the live site.
- Live sync over the real public relays: a change made on one phone-sized browser reached a second one in
  1.6 to 1.8 s.
- Released APK installed and opened on Android 14 and Android 11 emulators: 9/9 checks each, sample trip
  opened, live sync connected from inside the app ("2 of 3 relays"), no crash in the device log.
